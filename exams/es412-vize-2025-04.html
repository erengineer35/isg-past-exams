Occupational Health and Safety II

ES 412 OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY II MIDTERM EXAM- APRIL 2025

This examination is comprised of 25 multiple-choice questions.
Each question is followed by four possible answers, of which only one is correct.
The maximum time for this examination is 30 minutes.

  • Kâğıtta işaret yok; tüm cevaplar çözülmüştür.
  • Son sayfadaki optik form talimat tablosu (bölüm kodları) alınmadı.
