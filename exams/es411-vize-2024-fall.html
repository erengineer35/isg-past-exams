Occupational Health and Safety I

ES 411 OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY I – MIDTERM EXAM – FALL 2024

This examination is comprised of 10 multiple-choice questions. Each question is followed by three or four possible answers, of which only one is correct. The maximum time for this examination is 30 minutes.

  • Başlık sayfası fotoğraflarında sol kenar kesik; talimat cümlesi ve senaryo metninin kesik baş harfleri bağlama göre tamamlandı. 'produces ?al components' sözcüğü okunamadı.
  • W 06/78 (S8-10) ve W 08/77 (S3-7) aynı sınava ait: aynı başlık, 'a)' şık stili, 10 soruluk kesintisiz numaralandırma (S1-2 başlık sayfası, S3-7, S8-10). Ağustos 2023 vizesindeki S8-9-10 ile numara çakışır ama o sınav 25 soru ve farklı içerik; ayrı sınav.

0 / 10 cevaplandı
Please read the scenario and answer the question based on that.
Scenario: You are an occupational safety specialist working for a medium-sized manufacturing company that produces [okunamadı]al components for the automotive industry. The workplace is classified as hazardous according to Law No. 6331. During a routine inspection, you observe the following:
1. Workers in the welding section are not wearing the required personal protective equipment (PPE) such as gloves and safety goggles.
2. The company has not conducted a formal risk assessment in the past year, even though several new machines were installed six months ago.
3. One worker mentions that they were asked to operate the new machinery without any specific training related to its safety.
4. The emergency evacuation plan is outdated and has not been practiced in over a year.
5. There is no designated occupational physician employed, even though the company has 150 employees.

1. Question: Based on Law No. 6331, which of the following actions must the employer take to comply with legal requirements?

  • I. Conduct a risk assessment to evaluate the hazards associated with the new machinery.
  • II. Ensure all workers receive appropriate safety training for the equipment they operate.
  • III. Update and regularly practice the emergency evacuation plan.
  • IV. Employ an occupational physician since the workplace is classified as hazardous with more than 50 workers.
  • V. Require workers to purchase their own personal protective equipment.

Doğru cevap: A · Çözüm (kaynakta işaret yok, doğrulanmamış) · IV doğru (tehlikeli sınıf, 50+ çalışan: işyeri hekimi zorunlu); V yanlış (KKD'yi işveren sağlar). Senaryo metninde bir sözcük okunamadı (passage).

2. What is the primary goal of ergonomics?

Doğru cevap: B · Çözüm (kaynakta işaret yok, doğrulanmamış)

3. Which of the following statements are correct regarding Occupational Physicians and Occupational Safety Specialists?

  • I. Occupational safety specialists are only accountable to the Ministry and not the employer.
  • II. Occupational physicians must notify the Ministry if employers fail to address life-threatening hazards.
  • III. A C-Class certification allows safety specialists to work in very hazardous enterprises.
  • IV. Occupational physicians are not required to have any certification to practice in this field.
  • V. Safety specialists must guide employers on OSH measures and report unresolved critical hazards to authorities.

Doğru cevap: B · Çözüm (kaynakta işaret yok, doğrulanmamış) · I yanlış (işverene de karşı sorumlu), III yanlış (C sınıfı az tehlikeli), IV yanlış (belge zorunlu).

4. Which of the following is NOT a type of ergonomic study?

Doğru cevap: C · Çözüm (kaynakta işaret yok, doğrulanmamış) · Q4 a), Q5 d), Q7 b) yanında silik kalem çizgisi var; işaret olarak alınmadı.

5. Which design approach is considered the most inclusive but also costly?

Doğru cevap: A · Çözüm (kaynakta işaret yok, doğrulanmamış)

6. Which of the following statements are relevant regarding Steps for Ergonomic Designs?

  • I. Ergonomic design focuses on fitting the worker to the task instead of fitting the task to the worker.
  • II. A key step in ergonomic design is deciding on the body part to accommodate, such as popliteal height for chair design.
  • III. Ergonomic design requires accommodating only the average user for cost efficiency.
  • IV. Deciding on the user population, such as children or older adults, is critical in ergonomic design.
  • V. Ergonomic design never includes considerations for adjustability.

Doğru cevap: A · Çözüm (kaynakta işaret yok, doğrulanmamış) · I ters (iş insana uydurulur), III ve V yanlış.
